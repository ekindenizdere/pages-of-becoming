Closed loop. The body runs the full circle; the head at the top takes the tail into its mouth, so the line never breaks. That is the only promise the drawing makes: it goes round.

Softened, not dulled. Each palette keeps the reference's variety (six to eight hues, scattered tile by tile) but lifts every tile towards the paper. Grey outlines carry the mosaic; nothing on the ring is darker than the text that may sit on it. "Colour strength" moves between the reference's brightness (120%) and almost tone-on-tone (20%).

Sisyphus. Inside the circle, feet on the serpent's back, leaning into the stone. At the top he hangs upside down; the loop has no summit. The cursor drives him, per the enactive rule; "loop" lets him go on his own.
