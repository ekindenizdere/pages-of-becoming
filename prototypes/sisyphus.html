The loop, engraved

A figure pushes a stone along the back of a serpent that bites its own tail. Nothing is drawn on the paper; everything is pressed into it. Four treatments, one motion. Move the cursor over the stage to drive him.

cursor drives the loop · idle: slow drift

Ground

Motion

What the four share. One motion: the figure walks the outside of the loop, always leaning into the stone, so at the bottom of the circle he pushes upside down. That is the point: there is no top of this hill. The serpent's tail passes into its mouth at the top right, where the figure's climb begins again.

Enactive by default. In "cursor" mode the visitor's hand moves him: left to right across the stage is one lap. Left alone he drifts, slowly. "Loop" runs on its own at the chosen pace. Reduced-motion settings hold him still.

Where it could go. As the hero of a piece about repetition, as the 404 page (the loop that never resolves), or as the opening of the home at a far smaller scale, pressed into the stone ground Eli already uses.
